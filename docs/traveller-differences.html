Classic Traveller: differences from the book

This page assumes you already know the book. It lists only where the generator departs from it.

Book
Traveller Book 3: Worlds and Adventures, Game Designers’ Workshop. The edition is not identified: it is either the 1977 original or the 1981 revision.
Rules implemented
The World Creation section, and world occurrence for subsector maps
Compared against
mapemounde library 0.5.0, as at 8 October 2026

Differences

  • The generator follows the book’s rolls and modifiers; there are no changes to them.
  • Rich trade classification: population 6, 7 or 8 qualifies, as the book’s “6 through 8” says. Atmosphere must be 6 or 8.

Not implemented

  • Travel zones: every world is Green. Amber and Red zones are left to the referee, as in the book.
  • Communications routes, world names and capital designations.
  • Additional planets and satellites in a system.
