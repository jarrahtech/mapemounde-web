BattleTech: differences from the book

This page assumes you already know the book. It lists only where the generator departs from it.

Book
BattleTech: Campaign Operations, Catalyst Game Labs, © 2016–2021 The Topps Company, Corrected Third Printing.
Rules implemented
The Solar System Generation chapter
Compared against
mapemounde library 0.5.0, as at 8 October 2026

Differences

  • Hot star option: on the Realistic column, a roll of 12 (F*) moves to the Hot Stars column on a further 1 in 6 chance. The book leaves this to the player.
  • Life zone edges: calculated from blackbody distances (345 K inner, 240 K outer), not read from the Primary Solar Stats Table. They differ slightly from the printed values (G2V is 0.666–1.376 AU here, 0.66–1.34 AU in the book).
  • Jump point distance: calculated from the star’s gravity (a safe limit of 0.000058 m/s²), not read from the book’s table.
  • Trace gases: each trace gas on an uninhabitable world is 1D3 percent, not 1D6 ÷ 2 percent.
  • Medium and small moons: sized from the book’s size ranges (100–500 and 1–100), not its dice formulas, and the result is used as a radius in kilometres.
  • Asteroid belt members: generated as Dwarf Terrestrial planets, so they take that type’s day length and density.
  • Satellite placement: rings take the innermost orbital slots, then moons in decreasing order of size. The book gives no placement order.

Not implemented

  • Year length (orbital period).
  • The Special Features and Occupancy table.
  • The optional rules: eccentric orbits, axial tilt, brown dwarfs, multiple stars, variable stars, realistic planetary placement and “Water Water Everywhere”.
  • Colony Creation (population and USILR codes).
